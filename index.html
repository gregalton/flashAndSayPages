Flash & Say

Short, friendly practice for reading words and phrases quickly and accurately.

How it works: a word or short phrase flashes on the screen for a moment. Take it in with one look, then type it (or, if a grown-up turns it on, say it out loud). The app gently adjusts the timing so most answers are successes, and longer phrases unlock as reading gets quicker.

Coming soon to the App Store

Confidence first

Every session starts with easy warm-ups and ends on a success. Feedback is encouraging and specific — no red X's, no fail sounds.

Grows with the reader

From single words to two-word, three-word and longer phrases, with flash time that adapts after each answer.

Your own progress

Streaks, personal bests and an optional feelings check-in. Readers are only ever compared with their own past sessions.

Comfortable to read

Supports Dynamic Type, adjustable letter spacing, calm background colors, Reduce Motion and VoiceOver. "Hear it" reads items aloud with the device's built-in voice.

For tutors & parents

A grown-ups area (behind a parental gate) sets the level and session length and creates a PDF progress report.

Private by design

No ads, no accounts, no analytics and no data collection. Progress stays on the device. Read the privacy policy.

Flash & Say is a practice tool. It is not a diagnosis, treatment or replacement for reading instruction or professional support.
