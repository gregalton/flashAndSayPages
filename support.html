Frequently asked questions

How do I open the Grown-ups area?

Tap the lock icon on the home screen and answer the multiplication question.

How do I answer by voice?

In Grown-ups, turn on “Allow answering by voice” and allow microphone and speech access. Voice answers need a device that can recognize speech on-device; otherwise the app keeps using typing, which always works.

The app heard me wrong.

Turn on “That was right” in Grown-ups so a helper can mark an answer correct, or simply type the answer.

How do I make text bigger or change spacing and colors?

Use “Look & sound” in the app. The app also follows your device's text size (Dynamic Type) and Reduce Motion settings.

How do I share progress with a tutor?

In Grown-ups, tap “Create PDF progress report”, then “Share report…”.

How do I reset progress?

In Grown-ups, tap “Reset all progress on this device”. Deleting the app also removes all its data.

Does the app collect data?

No. See the privacy policy.
